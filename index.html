Willmore candidates

The candidate family for the minimizers of the Willmore energy in a conformal class: three constrained Willmore tori, one for each half-period of the torus, and the tree of sinh-Gordon spectral data that leads to them, drawn in ℝ⁴ by the generalized Sym formula.

Genus

The surface is f = Q(u₁ᵀF(λ₁)) Q(u₂ᵀF(λ₂))⁻¹ in ℝ⁴ (the generalized Sym formula), from the eigenlines uj of the Killing field at two points λ₁, λ₂ of the spectral curve. The Sym points follow from the candidate or the point of the tree: a branch point that is a Sym point is drawn blue, and any other Sym point is a blue square λj (on the unit circle a σ-node, elsewhere a point of a resonance pair not yet on the circle).

Drag a branch point to move along the tree. In genus 2 the curve determines the internal τ and the scale t, so the tree is recomputed for that τ and the dot goes to the point at that t; while dragging, candidate 2 of the dragged curve is drawn. In genus 1 the internal τ is not determined by the curve, and dragging moves along the genus 1 edge. Shift-drag turns everything together. Double-click to add or remove a branch point (this leaves the tree).
